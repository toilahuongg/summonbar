# Code of Conduct

Summonbar follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming and respectful, give and take feedback gracefully, and focus on what's best for the project and the people in it. Harassment, insults, personal attacks, and publishing others' private information are not acceptable, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project lives.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour bothers you, contact the maintainer privately through [GitHub](https://github.com/toilahuongg). Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits and issues, and may block contributors who don't follow this code.
